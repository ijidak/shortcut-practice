⌨️ Shortcut Trainer

Pick an app, hit Start, and race the 5-second clock. Get it right twice to retire it.
Heads-up on browser-reserved keys: A few combos are grabbed by your browser/OS before the page sees them (e.g. ⌘T new tab, ⌘N new window, ⌘⇧]/⌘⇧[ and ⌘1–⌘9 switch browser tabs). They may still open/switch a browser tab. ⌘W and ⌘Q are intentionally excluded so you never lose the game. Everything else is suppressed by the trainer. For the smoothest run, use this in a dedicated browser window.
